Tab bar with favourites: what gives way?

Tab bars drawn to scale (15 mm tabs, 12 mm controls, 1 mm spacing) for the 10.1 in panel at both resolutions. Yellow = favourite view tabs, dashed = the reserved brightness and MOB spaces (empty today, plan 5 and phase 2). Fixed tabs stay; favourites take what is left. Click the one you want, or say something else in the terminal.

A

Tracks and Settings stay as tabs

Favourites sit above Views, Tracks, Settings. Reserved spaces give way to favourites until brightness and MOB exist. 3 favourites fit at both sizes (1 at 1024x600 and 2 at 1280x800 once brightness and MOB arrive).

Views page: star = favourite (shown as a tab)

Either way: a favourite's tab shows its short name (you type it, about 6 characters at the current tab font). The Views page tile holds the full name. Hold a tile for Edit, Rename, Favourite, Duplicate, Delete. If more favourites are starred than fit, the extras are plain views, with a note on the tile.

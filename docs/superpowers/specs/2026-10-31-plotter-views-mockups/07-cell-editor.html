Editing a cell view

Grid rule: up to 2 columns, up to 3 rows, a cell may span both columns, 6 cells max. That gives 14 possible layouts. Two ways to edit. Click one, or describe another.

A

Tap a cell, change it in place

Edit mode outlines the cells. Tap one: a half-height sheet with its field list plus layout buttons that act on that cell: Split (one cell becomes two side by side) / Merge (two in the row become one), Add row below, Remove row. Buttons grey out at the limits. Same shape as the slot sheet, no gallery to learn.

B

Pick the layout from a gallery, then fill the cells

Setup sheet gets a Layout row: tap it, a gallery of the 14 shapes as thumbnails. Pick one, then tap each cell for its field. Shows every possibility at once; fields are re-asked when the cell count changes.

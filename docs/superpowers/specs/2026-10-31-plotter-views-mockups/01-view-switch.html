Where does the view switch live?

Garmin: Home, Pinned, pick a tile (3 taps). Each option below is the chart page as it is today on the left, and what the switch tap opens on the right. Tab bar stays on the right edge, 12 mm floor, 1024x600 and 1280x800. Click the one that feels right, or describe something else in the terminal.

A

Views tab opens a tile grid (2 taps)

Chart and Numbers tabs merge into one Views tab. Tap it: a grid of your named views, one tile each, plus New. Tap a tile: that view fills the page. Views tab label could show the current view's name.

B

Each view is its own tab (1 tap)

Saved views sit directly in the tab bar above Tracks and Settings. One tap switches. Room for about 3 views at 1024x600 and 4 at 1280x800 before tabs run out; more views need a scroll or an overflow tile. Names must be short.

C

Garmin-style Home (2 taps, slim tab bar)

Tab bar shrinks to Home, Lock, Day, so the chart gets more width. Home is a page of big tiles: your views first, then Tracks, Settings, About. Tap a view tile to show it. Closest to the Garmin, fewest things on the chart edge.

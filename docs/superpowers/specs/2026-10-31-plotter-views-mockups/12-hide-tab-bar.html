Hiding the tab bar

Yes, it is possible and cheap: the bar animates to a grip, the view takes the width. The question is how it goes away and comes back. All options keep a 12 mm wide grip on the right edge so Lock and the palette are one tap further, never gone. Click one or describe another.

A

Tap the grip to hide, tap the grip to show

A narrow grip (12 mm wide, full height, a chevron) sits between the view and the bar. Tap it: bar slides out, chart widens, grip stays with the chevron flipped. Tap again: bar back. The state is remembered per view (a fishing view can start hidden) and across boots.

B

Auto-hide after a few seconds, tap the grip to peek

Bar shows for 8 s after any tap on it, then slides away to the grip. Tap the grip: bar back for 8 s. Maximum chart almost all the time, no per-view setting. Downside: the bar moves on its own, and a tab you reach for may be mid-slide.

A setting picks the delay or turns auto-hide off (then it behaves like A).

C

Grip as in A, plus a per-view "Tab bar: shown / hidden" in Setup

Same grip and tap. The Setup sheet adds a row so a view can be saved with the bar hidden; switching to that view hides it, switching to one saved shown brings it back. The grip tap still works any time as an override until the next view switch.

A with the per-view choice made explicit. Most likely what you meant by "minimize"; say if the grip alone is enough.

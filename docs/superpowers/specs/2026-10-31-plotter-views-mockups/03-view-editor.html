Creating or editing a view

From Home, tap + New view (or hold a tile, Edit). Two shapes for the editor. Both end with the same slot sheet you have today (on/off, field, size from big lists, no keyboard). Tab bar shown with the icon-over-short-name tabs you asked for.

A

Edit in place (Garmin Edit Overlays)

The editor is the view, live, with a strip across the top. Strip: icon, short name, full name, background (Nav, Fish, None), Cancel, Done. Below it every slot is outlined; off slots show faintly with their position name. Tap a slot: the slot sheet. What you see is what you save.

B

Form first, then slots

A settings-style page: name, short name, icon, background, favourite, then a "Edit overlays" button that opens the in-place slot outline from A. Simpler strip, one more step to reach the slots. Good if the strip in A feels cramped at 1024x600.

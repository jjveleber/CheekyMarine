Sizing a slot on its own

You asked for each overlay sized individually and dynamically, not Small/Medium/Large. The size is one number per slot: digit height in mm (the label and unit scale with it; a banner's height or a bar's width follow from it). Clamped so the slot fits the space left (R51), floor 6 mm, ceiling what fits. Three ways to set it. Click one or describe another.

A

Drag a corner handle on the outlined slot

In edit mode, tap a slot to select it: a big round handle appears on its inner corner. Drag it, the digits grow and shrink live on the chart. Tap the slot again for the field sheet. Direct, like resizing a Garmin split. Needs a steady finger: editing is a dockside job, so pinch and drag are acceptable here even though the chart page bans them.
